## [0.1.0] - 2026-07-26

### Added

- PushPlus and QQ Mail notification channels.
- Escalating reminders from day 140 through day 180.
- Password-protected administration page.
- Signed confirmation and 24-hour snooze links.
- SQLite persistence, event history, heartbeat notifications, and daily backups.
- Docker Compose deployment configuration.
